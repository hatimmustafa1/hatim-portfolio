Hello! I’m Hatim Mustafa

Graphic designer with 4+ years of experience in corporate design, product marketing, and print media.
I turn ideas into clear, creative, and effective visual designs that connect with people and communicate the right message.

Skilled in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva, with hands-on experience in AI-assisted design, photography, and video production to create content that informs, inspires, and leaves a lasting impression.

My approach balances creative ideas with practical communication. I focus on strong visual hierarchy, thoughtful typography, and consistent brand systems so every design feels purposeful, polished, and easy to understand.
